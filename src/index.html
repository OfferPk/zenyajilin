Rules of Yajilin (ヤジリン)

🌸 The Goal

Weave a single unbroken, non-intersecting closed loop through the grid cells while discovering the locations of hidden black stones.

1. Arrow Clue Lanterns

Clue tiles contain an arrow and a number (e.g. 1 →). The number indicates the exact count of black stones in that direction extending to the grid border. Clue tiles are fixed obstacles and never visited by the loop.

2. No Adjacent Black Stones

Two black stones never touch orthogonally (they cannot share an edge). Diagonal touching is permitted.

3. Single Continuous Closed Loop

Every cell that is neither an arrow clue nor a black stone must be visited by exactly one closed loop. The loop cannot branch, self-cross, or form separate mini-loops.

💡 Controls & Tips

• In Loop Mode, tap or drag between adjacent cells to connect loop tracks.

• In Stone Mode, tap a cell to place a black stone (●).

• In Dot Mode, tap a cell to mark it as confirmed open path (·).

• Arrow clue tiles turn Green when satisfied!
