## Full Description (4,000 characters max)
Step into the tranquil meditation of an authentic Japanese Zen rock garden with ZenYajilin, an elegant logic puzzle based on the acclaimed Nikoli pencil puzzle Yajilin (ヤジリン / Yajirin, "Arrow Ring").

In ZenYajilin, your objective is to weave a single unbroken, non-intersecting closed loop through serene stone courtyards, guided by numbered stone arrow lanterns that illuminate hidden black stones.

=== THE RULES OF YAJILIN ===
1. Arrow Clue Lanterns: Clue tiles contain a directional arrow and a number (e.g. 1 →, 2 ↓). The number indicates the exact count of black stones along that ray extending to the grid border. Clue tiles are fixed obstacles and are never visited by the loop.
2. No Adjacent Black Stones: Two black stones never touch orthogonally (they cannot share a horizontal or vertical edge). Diagonal touching is permitted.
3. Single Unbroken Closed Loop: Every cell that is neither an arrow clue nor a black stone must be visited by exactly one continuous closed loop. The loop moves strictly orthogonally through cell centers, cannot branch, cannot self-cross, and cannot form isolated mini-loops.
